Key Finding

Defined Benefit Pension participants are 72% retirement-ready, versus just 22% for 401(k) and 10% for Annuity holders. Account-based plans shift longevity risk to the participant, and the readiness gap widens despite comparable funded ratios (~86%). The leverage point is contribution rate.
